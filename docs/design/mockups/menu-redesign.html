Proposed mission map and results screens

Both screens rebuilt around one idea: a single cursor over a grid of real cells, driven equally by keys, pad and pointer. These are live — click the rows and cards to drive them.

Mission map

The route becomes a list, because the route is a list. The half of the screen that was decorative honeycomb becomes mission identity, and the lock reason moves onto the card where you can read it before committing.

Results and upgrades

The run's payoff becomes four tiles instead of a run-on line. The shop becomes cards you can point at. The permanent fork gets its own panel, its own warning, and a confirmation step.
